## S2 reproduced, and narrowed

The witness is exact.  For `h2_2-6_p2_act` menu 3, token 1, over parameters
`[y, x, t1, u1]` at denominator 1, the token vectors are

```text
a = [-3,  3,  1,  2]      six candidate carriers, determinants
b = [ 1, -3, -3,  1]      (y,x) 6   (y,t1) 8   (y,u1) -5
c = [ 2,  0,  2, -3]      (x,t1) -6 (x,u1) 9   (t1,u1) 7
```

so the minimising carrier is `(y,u1)` with `det = -5`; the permitted numeric
choice `x = t1 = 24` gives `c = (96,-144)` and `adj(M)c = (384,336)`, and
neither coordinate is divisible by 5.  Choosing `Lambda` in a finer lattice
cannot help, because it does not touch `c`.  The step is false as written.

Two further computations narrow the gap, and both are new:

* **A unimodular carrier removes the difficulty entirely**, since then there is
  nothing to divide.  5,491 of the 7,963 tokens admit one, and at the level of
  contexts, where the family is actually chosen, **2,616 of the 2,789**
  contexts that occur have a family all of whose tokens admit one.  Adding
  "prefer a family with unimodular carriers" to the choice rule confines the
  problem to **173 contexts**, for which the best achievable determinant is at
  most 36 and lies in `{2,3,4,6,8,9,24,36}`, of least common multiple 72.
* **The congruence route does not close by itself.**  The reachable set of
  `adj(M) rhs` modulo `d` is the subgroup generated by
  `{M adj(M) v_p mod d}` over the non-carrier columns.  `scripts/carrier_integrality.py`
  finds that for **2,472 tokens this is a proper subgroup at every admissible
  carrier**, so the resolving cell's own numeric parameters cannot always clear
  the determinant.

There is also a structural remark worth recording.  The value a cell passes to
its parent is its head, and by (E5) the head is an independent parameter, so a
coarsened lattice cannot propagate upward *except* when the carrier contains
the head or through the rigid rows.  3,115 of the 4,904 tokens that have a head
parameter do have it in their minimising carrier, but a head-free carrier
exists for a family of 2,744 of the 2,789 contexts.  S2 and S4 therefore have
the same root cause: the cells where (E5) fails or is given up.

## S1: the blind spot is real, the collision is not

The gate enumerates catalogue contexts; the ray is a constructor template and
is not one; the enumeration records exactly seven realizable pairs in which the
ray is the parent; none was ever evaluated.  That much is correct and is now
closed by a new closure condition C7.

The audit's collision, however, was computed against the single rigid row
`(-3x/2; -x/2, -x, x/2; x)`, every label of which is a multiple of the input.
That row is a specialisation.  The family the constructor uses has two
parameters,

```text
(-s-2t;  -t,  -s-t,  s;  2t),      x = 2t,
```

so the head is `-s-x` with `s` free and the only label depending on the input
alone is `-x/2`.  Against that family the seven pairs come out as: five
children pass outright (`h0_3_p1_bot`, `h1_1_p0_act2`, `h2_3_p1_bot` have no
head-only label at all; `h2_1_p0_act2` has normalised ratio `-1`, which is not
`-1/2`), `h0_1-2_p0_bot` passes through its head-multiplier-one menu entry, and
`h0_1_p0_act2` and `h0_2_p0_act2` are the direct-edge two-input rows, whose
labels depend on two inputs and cannot be identically a multiple of the
parent's single input.  C7 reports 7 admissible, 0 open, and C1--C7 pass.

## S2: right, and sharper than stated

Two of the audit's premises can be improved and one cannot.

*The residue-two ray is not rigid* (see the main adjudication): (E5) holds
there and no head propagates through it.

*Of the two direct-edge two-input rows, only one is rigid.*  Enumerating every
bijection between labels and outputs for the residual-$\{2\}$ topology leaves a
family with a direction along which the head moves with both inputs held fixed,
so (E5) holds there as well.  For the residual-$\{1\}$ topology the same
enumeration leaves exactly one nondegenerate family and its head is `-x1-x2`.

*That one row is genuinely unbounded.*  It has no token, so it neither resolves
a pending token nor stops at one, and the scheduler does produce chains of them
of length `Theta(D)` on a comb.  A head becomes symbolic only at an owner whose
carrier contains its input, and 2,642 of the 2,789 contexts that occur have a
family whose tokens all admit a carrier avoiding both head and inputs, so the
propagation can start only at the remaining 147; but that is a narrowing, not a
bound.

The consequence is what the audit says.  The symbolic support is `(3+L)W` with
`L` the chain length, `|Phi|` and the boxes are `O(D^2)`, and the magnitudes
are `O((D+1)^2)`.  **The threshold linear in `D` is therefore not established;
what the present argument gives is the same statement with `(D+1)` replaced by
`(D+1)^2`.**  The manuscript now says this.

## The reset, found 2026-09-04

Item 1 below is closed as a statement about the decomposition.  Joining two
consecutive residual-$\{1\}$ two-input rows into one cell gives a topology with
nine labels and nine outputs; enumerating every bijection between them leaves
**14 nondegenerate families, and 12 of them have a direction along which the
head moves with every input held fixed**.  One of the 12, at an integer point:

```text
g0 = 14   r0a0x0 =  13   r0a0x1 = -29
g1 = -16  r1a0x0 =  -9   r1a0x1 =  22
s0 =  3   x1 = -3        x2 = 19
```

with `P = O` and the nine labels distinct and nonzero, a solution space of
dimension five, and the head-free direction equal to `g0` itself, so the head
may be moved on its own.  For comparison the single row, run through the same
program, gives **1 family and 0 with a free head**, which is the rigidity the
third audit relies on.

A chain is therefore cut into two-level joints, each with a free head, and an
odd chain leaves one row at the top whose parent is not such a row.  The
symbolic support returns to three cells and one row, the boxes and the
magnitudes are linear in `D+1` again, and the threshold linear in `D` is
restored.  `scripts/twoinput_reset.py` is the computation and
`data/twoinput_reset.json` the certificate.

What this does **not** yet do: our scheduler does not emit these joints, so the
lemma is a statement about the decomposition that the regression does not
exercise, and the joint is not in the catalogue, so the gate does not evaluate
it either.
