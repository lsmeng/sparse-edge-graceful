## S1 reproduced

returns exactly one owner and it is

```text
owner 8 -> h0_4_p0_act2
  arms            [{child 3, length 4, role residual}]
  ports           0
  neutral units   none
  active edges    child 13 role kept_act2_1 (L11 passthrough)
                  child 16 role kept_act2_2 (L11 passthrough)
```

so two active children are kept at residual `{4}`, and neither is a free-head
sibling.  Appendix A's prose allows two kept children only at portless residual

so the machine-checked part is unaffected; the defect is that Appendix A, which

## S2 reproduced, and narrowed

The witness is exact.  For `h2_2-6_p2_act` menu 3, token 1, over parameters
`[y, x, t1, u1]` at denominator 1, the token vectors are

```text
a = [-3,  3,  1,  2]      six candidate carriers, determinants
b = [ 1, -3, -3,  1]      (y,x) 6   (y,t1) 8   (y,u1) -5
c = [ 2,  0,  2, -3]      (x,t1) -6 (x,u1) 9   (t1,u1) 7
```

so the minimising carrier is `(y,u1)` with `det = -5`; the permitted numeric
choice `x = t1 = 24` gives `c = (96,-144)` and `adj(M)c = (384,336)`, and
neither coordinate is divisible by 5.  Choosing `Lambda` in a finer lattice
cannot help, because it does not touch `c`.  The step is false as written.

Two further computations narrow the gap, and both are new:

* **A unimodular carrier removes the difficulty entirely**, since then there is
  nothing to divide.  5,491 of the 7,963 tokens admit one, and at the level of
  contexts, where the family is actually chosen, **2,616 of the 2,789**
  contexts that occur have a family all of whose tokens admit one.  Adding
  "prefer a family with unimodular carriers" to the choice rule confines the
  problem to **173 contexts**, for which the best achievable determinant is at
  most 36 and lies in `{2,3,4,6,8,9,24,36}`, of least common multiple 72.
* **The congruence route does not close by itself.**  The reachable set of
  `adj(M) rhs` modulo `d` is the subgroup generated by
  `{M adj(M) v_p mod d}` over the non-carrier columns.  `scripts/carrier_integrality.py`
  finds that for **2,472 tokens this is a proper subgroup at every admissible
  carrier**, so the resolving cell's own numeric parameters cannot always clear
  the determinant.

There is also a structural remark worth recording.  The value a cell passes to
its parent is its head, and by (E5) the head is an independent parameter, so a
coarsened lattice cannot propagate upward *except* when the carrier contains
the head or through the rigid rows.  3,115 of the 4,904 tokens that have a head
parameter do have it in their minimising carrier, but a head-free carrier
exists for a family of 2,744 of the 2,789 contexts.  S2 and S4 therefore have
the same root cause: the cells where (E5) fails or is given up.

## What has been repaired

* S3, the error budget: `epsilon` is now `delta/24` rather than `delta/6`, the
  hypothesis is `|Z_n \ X| <= delta n / 8`, and both perturbations are shown to
  land inside `delta n`.
* M2: the split uses floor and ceiling and the discrepancy is bounded by 12 and
  absorbed by that slack.
* M1: the handoff is now `X' = X \ (-T)` with the chosen block's size reduced
  by three and dropped when it becomes zero, and the complement bound raised
  by three.
* M4: Appendix B now opens on (E1)--(E5) plus the choice rule, not on a blanket
  (E1)--(E6).
* M5: the count is charged to the last nonzero coordinate of each form, which
  is what makes it valid; the text says so explicitly.
* N1, N2, N3: corrected.
* S2: the false step is withdrawn.  The manuscript now carries
  Remark~\ref{rem:integrality} stating what fails, the witness, what the two
  routes are, and the two computations above, and says plainly that until the
  173 contexts are dealt with the constant `A` is not established.
